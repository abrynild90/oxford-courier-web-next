Visual findings

  • Kennon’s south view shows four round columns, an open balustrade, the centered entrance and both patinated copper domes. The north view shows the pilasters and three upper windows. The farther north view clears the trees that blocked one dome from the south.
  • Huddle House’s round badge, rust panel and windows read from both parking-lot angles. The larger modeled wall still reads cooler and paler than the warmer tones in the exterior reference.
  • Lafayette Elementary’s three open arches have pale surrounds and curved soffits. The closer view shows the glazing set back from the arcade.
  • The Lamar Park portal view shows the roof edge. The walking-height shot beneath the canopy shows the new folded joists, girders and braces.
